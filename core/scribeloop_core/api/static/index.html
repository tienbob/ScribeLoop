How recording ends & what is saved
A dated record is created when you choose Record, then renamed from the notes. Call detection uses app audio activity. After 60 seconds of known inactivity, recording stops and notes are prepared. Stop and Keep recording remain available. Uncertain detection keeps recording instead of guessing that the call ended.


FIRST-USE AGREEMENT
Before your first recording
Use ScribeLoop only when you have permission to record the people captured. You choose Record or Not now for each detected call; accepting here does not start recording.
The selected microphone and meeting app audio are captured. Browser capture includes every tab in that browser. Cloud transcription sends audio to your selected provider. Automatic notes send transcript text and speaker labels to your configured summary endpoint. Longer calls use incremental section notes and a final combined summary, with at most 128 requests and 8 MB of request text per saved meeting across retries. Each request allows up to 4,000 output tokens. Provider charges apply; the selected recording preset displays a shared audio cost cap. Voice learning is a separate per-person preference and never enabled by naming. Explicit manual reruns upload the retained audio again. Provider details remain visible in recording preferences and meeting notes.
Saved audio, transcripts and notes stay in the app’s configured storage. Unarchived audio is deleted after 30 days while the app is running, idle and this agreement is accepted. Archive audio to keep it longer. Transcripts and notes remain after audio expiry. Exported files are readable plaintext outside app storage.
You can stop recording anytime or revoke this agreement in preferences to stop processing and block new requests. Revoking does not delete saved meetings. OS microphone and Screen Recording permissions are still required.
