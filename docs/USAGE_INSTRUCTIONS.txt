ScribeLoop — Installation & Usage
Internal test build 0.1.45 (46) · 6 November 2026

1. INSTALL

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), running macOS 13 Ventura or later.
Download ScribeLoop-latest-arm64.dmg from this folder (currently 0.1.45, build 46).
Open the DMG, drag ScribeLoop into Applications, then eject the disk image.
Open ScribeLoop from Applications. Python, Homebrew and Xcode are not required.

This internal build is not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks it, first confirm the
download came from your trusted distributor, then follow Apple's app-specific
Open Anyway instructions: https://support.apple.com/102445
Company-managed Macs may require IT approval. Do not disable Gatekeeper.

Updating? Quit ScribeLoop completely, including its menu-bar app, before replacing
the copy in Applications. Keep only one installed copy. Updates normally preserve
your workspace; do not downgrade an existing database.

2. FIRST SETUP

Open Settings/Preferences using the gear icon. Most configuration lives here.

Cloud setup (simplest):
- Models: choose Deepgram for transcription and enter your own Deepgram API key.
- Choose a supported notes provider, model and its API key.
- Recording: search and check the languages you expect, such as English and
  Vietnamese. Select a few relevant languages rather than every available one.
- Add useful names or vocabulary one phrase per line, spelled as spoken.
- Save changes. Provider usage is billed to your own account.

Local setup (optional):
- Install Ollama separately and choose Ollama · Local under Models.
- Enter an audio-capable local model; text-only models cannot transcribe.
- Use Check Ollama and wait for its result. Model downloads can be several GB.
- Choose Ollama for notes too if you want all model processing on your Mac.
- Prepare/download the models while online before using the app offline.
- Local transcription starts AFTER recording ends; there is no live transcript.
- Optional local speaker labeling requires a separate diarization add-on.

Grant microphone access when requested. Capturing meeting-app/system audio also
requires Screen Recording / Screen & System Audio Recording permission in macOS
System Settings → Privacy & Security. Use Check capture access without recording
to check access silently. Calendar permission is optional.

3. RECORD AN ONLINE CALL

- Get permission from everyone whose audio you will capture.
- Choose New recording, then select the meeting app/browser and microphone.
- Review the processing agreement and press Record & take notes.
- On macOS 14.2+, ScribeLoop can suggest a possible call; approve its recording
  popup to start. On macOS 13–14.1, start and stop manually.
- Browser capture may include sound from other tabs in that browser.
- Use Pause and Continue to suspend and resume capture.
- Press Stop recording when finished. Call-end detection may first show a
  60-second countdown. Choose Keep recording if the meeting is still active.
- If a browser remains active after a call ends, stop manually.

For an in-person meeting, choose a microphone-only recording instead.
In call transcripts, microphone bubbles appear on the right and meeting audio
on the left. Speaker labels are anonymous; you can rename people manually.
Pause/Continue, speaker bubbles and language selection are also available for
supported recording flows.

4. REVIEW TRANSCRIPT AND NOTES

Wait for transcription and notes processing to finish after Stop. Open the saved
conversation and use Notes, Transcript or Audio. Review important names, numbers
and decisions: recognition and generated notes can be wrong.

Use Latest in the Transcript tab to return to the newest speech after scrolling
back. Name people to make the conversation easier to follow. Notes include source
links that lead back to the supporting transcript.

If notes fail but the transcript is saved, use Generate notes to retry after
checking the configured notes model/API key. Retrying cloud processing may cost
money. You can customize the notes prompt in Settings.

Audio playback is optional and produces sound when you press Play.
Recording/exporting alone does not play audio.

5. CALENDAR

- First add your work calendar account to Apple's Calendar app on this Mac.
- In ScribeLoop, open Settings → Calendar, connect, allow access and select the
  calendars to display. No calendars are selected by default.
- Open Calendar in the sidebar to see Today/Upcoming meetings.
- Open meeting launches a supported meeting link in its normal app/browser;
  it does not join a call for you or begin recording.
- An event needs a supported meeting URL in its URL, location or notes. Events
  without a meeting link can still be used for in-person meetings.
- To associate a saved recording, use Meeting → Link calendar event….
- Calendar selection, refresh and Disconnect Calendar are in Settings → Calendar.

6. SHARE BY EMAIL

Finish recording/processing, then open the conversation and choose Email or
Meeting → Share by email….

- Summary is selected by default and goes into the message body.
- Check Transcript to attach the text transcript.
- Check Audio to attach the retained recording. Both attachments can be selected.
- Include source references is OFF by default for a clean summary. Turn it on
  to include citation numbers and a Sources section. In-app sources stay intact.
- Edit the subject and optionally use Preview.
- Click Open email draft once. Your mail app opens with the selected content
  attached automatically. Choose recipients, review, then press Send yourself.

A working mail account must be configured in your mail app. If handoff fails,
use Save… or Copy text. Attachments above 15 MB combined must be saved and shared
using your mail app's own options. Exported/shared copies are readable files.
Copies handed to the mail app are kept for seven days, then cleaned up on a
later app use; copies you explicitly save remain where you save them.

7. PERMISSIONS & TROUBLESHOOTING

Capture denied even though its toggle is on after an update?
Quit ScribeLoop. In macOS Privacy & Security, refresh ONLY ScribeLoop's recording
permission entry for /Applications/ScribeLoop.app, approve locally, and reopen.
Never send anyone your Mac password or API keys.

Calendar missing? Connect/request access inside ScribeLoop first, choose calendars,
and confirm that Apple's Calendar app has synced the events. If denied, review
Calendar access in macOS Privacy & Security.

Poor transcript? Check the input microphone and level/noise settings, select the
expected languages, and add specific names/phrases. Optional Refine transcript
after recording adds a billed Deepgram pass; it does not guarantee correctness.

This is an internal preview. Automatic call detection/stopping depends on the
meeting app and macOS. Manual New recording and Stop remain available.

8. DATA & BUG REPORTS

Each Mac has its own encrypted workspace and Keychain credentials. The installer
does not include another user's recordings or API keys. There is no account sync
or supported workspace transfer. Cloud transcription sends audio to the selected
provider; cloud notes send transcript text to the notes provider. Fully local
processing requires local models for BOTH transcription and notes.

Audio normally expires after 30 days unless archived; transcripts/notes remain.
Email attachments and saved exports are outside the encrypted workspace.

Report: app version, Mac model/macOS version, audio source/call app, exact error,
and steps to reproduce. Mention whether you pressed Stop or it stopped itself.
Do not include passwords, API keys or private recordings without prior agreement.
